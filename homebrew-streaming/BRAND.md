# Brand Thesis

> **OSS-heavy, future-proof, Factorio-brain Claude orchestrator who builds in public.**

That's the one-sentence promise. Pin to channel banner, video descriptions, every bio.

---

## What that actually means (the unpacking)

**OSS-heavy:** I default to open-source. When a $25 paid app and a free OSS equivalent exist, I build the OSS path AND show the build. Nothing I demonstrate is gated behind a vendor.

**Future-proof:** Every system I show is designed to survive 12+ months unattended. Plain text > GUI. Git > vendor cloud. Bash + standard Unix > brittle frameworks. The Grandchild-2076 test: would this still work for someone reading my repo in 50 years.

**Factorio-brain:** I think in belts, inserters, blueprints, throughput. Mental models from logistics games map onto AI orchestration cleanly. I use these metaphors on stream — they make complex pipelines instantly legible.

**Claude orchestrator:** I'm not a generic AI streamer. I work specifically with Claude Code + Claude API. Deep specialization > shallow breadth. Viewers know what they're getting.

**Builds in public:** Every artifact lives in a public GitHub repo. Every prompt I use, every script I write, every failure I autopsy — all clonable. The stream is the demo; the repo is the deliverable.

---

## What I'm NOT

- Not a "general AI news" channel (Theo, Fireship own that)
- Not a polished tutorial channel (those are scripted; I'm live)
- Not a beginner channel (I assume terminal comfort, git literacy, Mac familiarity)
- Not a rage-bait or take-machine channel (I make things; opinions emerge from making)

---

## Voice + style anchors

- Tier lists for everything (10× faster to scan than prose)
- Decision Gates when forking (table + my pick + why-not bullets)
- Forensic critique mode (rounds, autopsies, failure-mode tables)
- Bold the scannable words
- Smart-14-year-old test for explanations
- Match the moment: warm when warm is needed, terse when terse is needed
- Frustration signals respected ("..." = caught it, recalibrate)

---

## Differentiation thesis

The AI streaming space already has Theo (frontend takes), Primeagen (vim/perf), Fireship (digestible news). The gaps:

1. **Nobody owns the "build your own version of every paid AI tool" niche.** That's me.
2. **Nobody streams with live whisper captions powered by their own pipeline.** That's me.
3. **Nobody open-sources every prompt + repo + skill they use, AS they use them.** That's me.
4. **Nobody runs a Factorio-mental-model OSS-purist Claude shop on YouTube.** That's me.

Four wedges, not one. Compounded, they're a moat.
